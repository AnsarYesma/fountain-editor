Fountain in 60 seconds

Fountain is the open plain-text screenplay format. Type plainly; these light rules do the formatting.

Scene headingLine starts with INT. / EXT. (or force any line with a leading .)
ActionJust write normally. Anything not matched below is action.
CharacterA line in ALL CAPS on its own, with dialogue beneath it. JORDAN (V.O.)
ParentheticalA line in (parentheses) under a character.
DialogueThe line(s) right below a character, no blank line between.
TransitionALL CAPS ending in TO:, or force with > at the start.
CenteredWrap in angle brackets: >THE END<
Emphasis*italic*   **bold**   _underline_
Page breakThree or more equals on a line: ===
Title pageAt the very top: Title: Credit: Author: then a blank line.
